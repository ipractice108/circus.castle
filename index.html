Before you write to us

Questions
we get daily

If yours isn't here, message us — replies come within 15–30 minutes during working hours.

I've never done aerial. Is it too late?
No. Adult classes start from the floor: conditioning, first climbs, first inversions, always with a coach within arm's reach. Most people hang upside down on day one and leave grinning.
Can I choose the apparatus?
Yes — classes are mixed apparatus. Tell the coach whether you want silks, hoop, straps or hammock and the session is built around it. Nobody is locked into one piece of equipment.
Is there a free trial?
Adults living in Bali get one free trial within seven days. Kids get two. Visitors on short trips book a walk-in class instead — 600 000 IDR, and it counts toward a pack if you continue.
Honestly, it looks expensive.
It buys more than floor time: an 8 m rig with a winch, coaches with state circus careers, a term calendar with a stage at the end of it, and photo sessions built into the programme. Pay three months up front and the price drops.
I don't have a scooter.
You don't need one. We're in the centre of Ubud — cafés, spa, market and the shops are a walk away, and so are we.
What if we miss a class or travel for two weeks?
One make-up class a month with 24 hours' notice. Cancel later than that and the class is spent. For illness, missed lessons are made up separately. Travelling for a fortnight? We fit the pass to your dates.
I'm a professional between contracts.
This is what the camp was built for. A room upstairs, the hall every day, full rig and winch, and nobody asking you to explain your level. Come for a week or stay for a season.
Where exactly are you?
Central Ubud, Bali. The exact address and a pin go out with your booking confirmation.
